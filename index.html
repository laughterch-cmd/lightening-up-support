Begin Again

A little space for your practice.

Meditation timer support for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Settle in

Choose the time between bells and the number of intervals, choose a sound, then tap Start. A five-second countdown gives you time to settle. A bell opens the session, bells mark the intervals, and two strikes mark the end.

Free and full timer

Sessions totaling up to 15 minutes are always free, as often as you like. All sounds, backgrounds, Apple Watch support, and optional Apple Health logging are included.

Full Timer Unlock is a one-time purchase for sessions up to eight hours on iPhone and Apple Watch. The purchase screen shows Apple's current price before you buy. There is no subscription, trial expiration, or account to create.

Total time is the interval length multiplied by the number of intervals. The countdown, pauses, and final sound decay are extra. Choose intervals every minute through one hour, then every five minutes through eight hours. You can set up to 480 one-minute intervals.

Restore your purchase

  1. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase for Media & Purchases.
  2. Open Begin Again on the device where you want access.
  3. Open Options (…) and Full Timer Unlock, then choose Restore Purchases.
  4. After restoration, close the purchase screen and tap Start when ready.

You can purchase or restore directly on Apple Watch without your iPhone nearby. Restoration needs an internet connection and access to Apple's store. Previously verified ownership can work offline through StoreKit's locally stored transactions. If verification fails, reconnect and restore. Free sessions remain available if the store is unavailable.

Cancelled purchases do not unlock the timer. Purchases awaiting approval unlock after Apple approves them. Refunds remove access to future longer sessions without interrupting a session already underway. Any paused longer session requires verified ownership before it can resume; restoration may be needed. Family Sharing is not enabled for this offering.

Lock Screen and long sessions

iPhone Live Activities have an eight-hour lifetime measured in wall-clock time. Countdown and pauses count toward that limit, even though they are not meditation time. Begin Again limits each display's lifetime and does not automatically restart an ended display.

Full eight-hour sessions can show a Live Activity, but it expires before the meditation ends because the countdown counts toward the display's time limit. Shorter sessions can also outlast their display after a long pause. The timer and audio run independently: open the app for current timing and controls. Apple Watch also provides Now Playing controls. An expired display may show “Open app for status” until it disappears.

Apple Watch audio

WatchOS chooses the audio output. Use the Watch speaker where supported, or Bluetooth headphones. Speaker playback is unavailable while charging and on some Watch models; connect headphones if asked. Raise the media volume if a bell is too quiet.

Watch playback uses long-form audio and cannot mix with other Watch audio. Other audio, calls, or removal of headphones may pause the session. Open the app and tap Resume when ready. iPhone playback can mix with other audio. Each device runs its own timer; sessions do not sync between devices.

Apple Health and privacy

Apple Health logging is optional and off by default. When enabled, completed sessions and sessions ended early after at least one minute can be saved as Mindful Minutes. Pauses and countdowns are excluded. Begin Again never reads your Health data.

The app has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics. Read the privacy policy.
